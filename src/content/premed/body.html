The Vision

The future of health
will not be shaped
by physicians alone.

It will be shaped by innovators, researchers, entrepreneurs, policymakers, technologists, and healthcare leaders working together to tackle humanity's greatest challenges.

The HUGEM Pre-Med Horizons Program at Harvard University introduces students to the biological foundations of health, the realities of modern healthcare systems, and the innovations transforming medicine.

Through interdisciplinary learning and collaborative problem-solving, students explore how they can contribute to building a healthier future for all.

Program Schedule

A week on campus.One capstone.

Each morning opens with a question. Each afternoon builds toward the capstone.

Day 01

Monday

Understanding Health

  • Human PhysiologyHow do the body’s major systems work together?
  • Evolutionary Perspectives on HealthHow has human biology adapted over time?
  • Health Throughout the LifecycleHow do health needs change from childhood through aging?
Day 02

Tuesday

Today’s Health Challenges

  • The Mental Health EpidemicWhat is driving rising mental health needs?
  • Social and Environmental DeterminantsHow do place, culture, and resources shape health?
  • Public Health Problem-Solving WorkshopHow can communities respond to complex health challenges?
Day 03

Wednesday

Understanding Healthcare

  • Patient-Centered CareHow can healthcare better reflect patient needs and experiences?
  • Quality of Healthcare in AmericaWhere does the system succeed, and where does it fall short?
  • America’s Healthcare Cost ChallengeHow can care become more affordable and accessible?
Day 04

Thursday

Building the Future

  • Data Science & AI for HealthHow are data and artificial intelligence changing medicine?
  • Vaccines and Public TrustHow can doctors communicate science and build confidence?
  • Healthcare Innovation LabStudents develop ideas for improving health and healthcare.
Day 05

Friday

Public Service Day

  • Morning – Community OutreachStudents partner with a local organization for hands-on service in the community.
  • Giving Back as a DoctorHow can physicians use their training to serve people beyond the clinic?
  • Public Service in MedicineExplore community health, public health, nonprofit medicine, and government service.
  • Community Impact WorkshopStudents connect service experiences to practical ways of improving health access.
Day 06

Saturday

Final Presentations & Awards (Half Day)

  • Final PresentationsTeams present their proposed solutions to a major healthcare challenge.
  • Awards & Certificate CeremonyRecognition for outstanding presentations, teamwork, and program achievement.
  • Closing AddressReflection on future pathways in medicine, health, and public service.

Behind the Program

HUGEM — teaching youth the responsibility of global citizenship.

Each year, HUGEM develops programs around the central theme of global citizenship — first, by helping students see how challenges in their own community intersect with the most pressing international issues, and second, by building the skills to act on them.

Leadership workshops, entrepreneurial pitch challenges, and pre-professional conferences combine learning from Harvard Trainers, hands-on problem-solving, and networking with peers — so students embrace their education and future careers with empathy, autonomy, and the ability to make an actionable impact locally, nationally, and internationally.

HUGEM Innovation Challenge (HIC)

Students research, stress-test, and pitch their own products, with personalized feedback from Harvard Trainers in venture capital and frontier technologies.

Workshops & Conferences

Leadership workshops, pitch challenges, and pre-professional conferences designed to build critical thinking and real-world impact.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before joining the Pre-Med Horizons Program at Harvard.

The program is open to students in grades 8–12 who are curious about medicine, healthcare, and the future of global health. No prior coursework is required.

The camp runs from 4 – 9 January 2027, in person at Harvard University in Cambridge, MA. Camp hours are 9 AM to 12 PM each day.

This is a day camp. Students attend sessions during camp hours and are not provided overnight accommodation.

The 1500 USD fee covers the full academic curriculum, instruction, lunch on program days, outings, interactive workshops, an official certificate, and all program materials.

Students explore topics such as healthcare systems, biomedical innovation, public health, and medical ethics, culminating in a team-based Future of Health Challenge capstone project.

Current Harvard undergraduates from HUGEM lead sessions and mentor students throughout the week, sharing their academic experiences and research interests.
