# One master brochure: Programme Calendar + School Partnership merged

The two uploaded PDFs cover the same 18 programmes for the same 2026-27 year, but split the story: the Calendar (16 pages) is the schools pitch with pathway spreads and a month-by-month start-date grid; the Partnership brochure (33 pages) adds outcome/evidence spreads, a grade matrix and a full page per programme for parents. Merging gives one document that a school can read in five minutes and forward to parents unchanged.

Two data conflicts show up between them and get resolved to one set of numbers throughout: cohort count (47 vs 41) and mentor/partner wording. The website catalogue (`src/lib/programmes.ts`) is the source of truth for grades, fees, session times and every start date, so the merged brochure is generated from it rather than retyped.

## Structure of the merged brochure (~34 pages)

**Part 1 — The schools case (pages 1-6)**
1. Cover: crimson/gold, headline stats (18 programmes, cohorts, 426+ published, 80+ countries), Book a partnership call.
2. Why schools partner with us: nothing on your timetable, every programme ends in something, mentors students recognise, whole-school entry points, easy for counsellors, your choice of depth — plus the "how to read this brochure" strip.
3. What students walk away with: the before/after framing and the four real student cards (Rouqaya, Swaraj, Kalina, Aisha) with parent quotes.
4. Outcomes by design: the six artefact types, the seven-pathway outcome table, and the "where it lands on an application" table (Common App / UCAS / IB CAS).
5. Every programme, one page: all 18 with pathway, grades, format, next start, fee.
6. Choose by grade: the grade-band matrix (Grade 5 through 12) with page references.

**Part 2 — Pathways (pages 7-13)**
One page per pathway (Medicine, Law/Debate/Communication, Finance, AI & Entrepreneurship, Research, Leadership & Global Affairs, Test Prep), each with its programme cards: outcome, grades, length, session times in Dubai and US Eastern, start dates, fee, live link.

**Part 3 — Plan the year (page 14)**
Every cohort month by month, September to April, colour-coded by pathway, with earlier cohorts marked as underway.

**Part 4 — Programme details for parents (pages 15-32)**
One full page per programme, kept from the partnership brochure: what the student does week by week, what they finish with, times in both zones, eligibility, fee, three-step next action ending in the Calendly link.

**Part 5 — Working with us (pages 33-34)**
Three partnership options (single cohort, curated calendar, co-branded pathway), the three-step process, the schools FAQ (fees, missed sessions, safeguarding), and a closing next-step page with contacts and the site link.

## Technical notes

- Generated by extending the existing `/tmp/br/gen.py` + `/tmp/br/pdf.py` pipeline (HTML/CSS to PDF via Playwright), reusing the v2 cover treatment and the crimson / ivory / antique gold / ink palette with the same serif + sans pairing. No app source files change.
- Programme data (grades, fees, session times, cohort dates, brochure links) is dumped from `src/lib/programmes.ts` so the brochure matches the live site; the cohort total is recomputed from that data and used consistently on the cover and in the schools copy.
- Narrative copy (why schools partner, artefact types, student stories, application-mapping table, partnership options, FAQ) is carried over from the two uploads, de-duplicated and edited to one voice.
- Links use `lwlschoolaccess.com` page URLs and `calendly.com/learnwithleaders`; the bird mark is embedded as a base64 data URI so rendering needs no network.
- Output: `/mnt/documents/LWL-School-Brochure-2026-27.pdf` (existing PDFs kept). Every page is then rendered to an image and visually checked for clipped text, table overruns, orphan headings and blank pages, with fixes re-rendered until clean.
