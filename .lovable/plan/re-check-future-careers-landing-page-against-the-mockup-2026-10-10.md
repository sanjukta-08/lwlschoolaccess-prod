1. Open `/masterclasses/future-careers-age-of-ai` at desktop and mobile widths and take screenshots of each section.
2. Compare each screenshot with the matching PDF page and the mockup HTML: header and hero, programme details, "why" and outcomes, challenge, Dr Jojo profile, interview and press, student notes, audience and schools, FAQ, reservation, footer.
3. Fix every difference in wording, order, spacing, colours, fonts (Playfair Display / Lato on this page only), images and buttons so the page matches exactly.
4. Leave the reservation buttons pointing to the outside portal. Don't build a form on this site, per the site rules.
5. Take new screenshots and confirm the page matches. Check that the rest of the site is unchanged.
